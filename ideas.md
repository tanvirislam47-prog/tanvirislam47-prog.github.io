# Chosen Direction — Signal Ledger

## Design Movement

**Technical Editorialism:** a synthesis of GitHub’s information density, a research notebook’s margin annotations, and the sober visual restraint of modern engineering publications.

## Core Principles

1. **Evidence before ornament:** Content hierarchy, honest status labels, and source-linked project work lead every decision.
2. **Measured technicality:** Lines, metadata, and code-like markers support scanning without imitating a hacker interface.
3. **Asymmetric reading rhythm:** Broad content panels alternate with narrow contextual rails, avoiding a generic centred marketing page.
4. **Progressive disclosure:** Deep project material opens only when requested, so the landing experience remains calm and legible.

## Color Philosophy

The site uses **graphite navy** as a stable research desk, **arctic blue** as the owned signal for links, status, and action, and low-contrast steel blue-grays for secondary structure. Near-white body text reduces glare while maintaining high readability; colour is intentionally scarce so verified work receives the strongest visual emphasis.

## Layout Paradigm

The page follows a **document-and-margin** layout: an anchored left identity rail on large screens, an editorial main column for narrative sections, and occasional metadata sidebars. On small screens, the rail becomes a compact header and the document sections reflow into a single reading sequence.

## Signature Elements

1. **Signal marks:** small blue squares and line indicators identify active items, research areas, and verified external links.
2. **Notebook labels:** monospace eyebrow labels such as `01 / research interests` clarify section purpose and create a coherent academic-document feel.
3. **System-grid dividers:** fine, low-contrast horizontal rules and small coordinate motifs provide structure without visual noise.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like handling a well-organized lab notebook: links underline decisively, project cards lift slightly, filters update immediately, and details expand in place with a clear route back. No interaction should suggest expertise beyond what the content verifies.

## Animation

Elements use short opacity and transform reveals on entry, with a maximum 220 ms transition and restrained stagger. Navigation, hover, and dialog motions use a crisp easing curve; the site honours reduced-motion preferences by removing non-essential movement.

## Typography System

**DM Sans** provides compact, readable body and interface text. **IBM Plex Mono** is reserved for metadata, labels, technology tags, and technical annotations. Headings use DM Sans at decisive but not oversized scales, with high-weight titles and generous tracking only for small labels.

## Brand Essence

**A research-oriented portfolio for an ICE student documenting a developing practice in intelligent and secure networked systems.**

**Personality:** methodical, curious, grounded.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are declarative and specific; calls to action are direct and low-pressure. Microcopy should clarify status instead of inflating it.

> “Exploring secure intelligence across networks, systems, and communication.”

> “Review selected project work”

## Wordmark & Logo

A simple **TLR signal monogram**: three linked geometric strokes that resolve into a subtle network node when viewed as a unit. The mark appears in arctic blue on graphite and as a stand-alone favicon; the name itself remains typographic rather than a default-font logo.

## Signature Brand Color

**Signal Blue — `#4EA1FF`**

## Style Decisions

- Decorative backgrounds must read as ledger grids, system traces, source marks, or technical diagrams rather than stars, constellations, or space imagery.
- On large screens, the identity resolves into a persistent document-and-margin structure: the TLR monogram, name, role status, and key metadata belong to an editorial rail rather than only a conventional top navigation.
- Signal Blue is reserved for actions, verified links, active markers, section coordinates, and key status signals. It must not function as general atmospheric glow.
